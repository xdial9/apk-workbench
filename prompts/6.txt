6.txt — STAGE 6: CLOSURE — SET YOUR AGENTS FREE
================================================================================
GOAL
Assume you missed something. Prove that you did not. This is the final stage:
adversarial self-audit, free exploration, verification/fixing, and a clean
accountable finish.

PHASE A — ADVERSARIAL AUDIT (structured; must complete)
For each question, answer in ai_analysis/attempt-01/memory/closure_audit.md
with citations:
  - Traversal: did Stage 1 truly cover 100% of the apktool/ tree? (cross-check
    against surface/files.jsonl; spot check directories not in your notes —
    do the deferred ones)
  - The ones that got away: any region you skipped as "boring"? Look again:
    assets, native .so strings, obscure res/ xml files, META-INF.
  - Cross-cutting misses: did any finding conclude WITHOUT its second
    half (e.g., endpoint found — was its source component ever traced via
    surface/links.jsonl? native export found — was its JNI/Java caller traced?)
  - Stage 2 disputes: is every cross_check.md dispute resolved, or
    explicitly carried as an open question?
  - Lead ledger completeness: every entry in lead_ledger.json has a status +
    reason?
  - Were there tool outputs you read only partially? (large surface/facts.jsonl
    sweeps or surface/shards-index.json shards you skipped; do a targeted
    re-sweep by fact kind or shard)
  - Anything in surface/location_map.json or trufflehog/findings-unverified.json
    you never opened or triaged?
Fix what the audit finds: open new mini-leads, settle them with Stage 3
method, reflect in ai_analysis/attempt-01/dossier.md, ai_analysis.json, and
lead_ledger.json.

PHASE B — FREE EXPLORATION LICENSE (your agents, your rules)
If your environment permits spawning agents/workers, fan out across
unresolved threads now (one thread each; report back into
ai_analysis/attempt-01/memory/). If not, iterate solo. Suggested free-hunting
grounds (non-exclusive):
  - decode anything still undecoded (base64, protobuf, custom encodings)
  - enumerate version deltas if sibling artifact folders are accessible
    from the workspace you were handed (otherwise skip conditionally)
  - string-mine native libs for endpoints/keys (cross-check native shards if
    surface/shards-index.json exists)
  - call-graph hunting: pick 3 sensitive APIs in surface/links.jsonl (or
    surface/static-analysis.json dex_analysis) and trace their call sites
    end-to-end; note where static mappings pay off.
Anything new -> new leads -> Stage 3 method -> reflect in deliverables.

PHASE C — CONVERGENCE (required stop condition; linear/finishable doctrine)
The stage is DONE only when ALL of these are true:
 [ ] every lead in ai_analysis/attempt-01/lead_ledger.json:
     pursued | dismissed (with reason) | open (with explicit reason)
 [ ] every coverage surface line in surface/static-analysis.json:
     expanded by your work or marked unknown/not_applicable with an honest
     reason
 [ ] ai_analysis/attempt-01/dossier.md + ai_analysis.json + lead_ledger.json
     updated with everything found in Phase A/B
 [ ] ai_analysis/attempt-01/closure_report.md written: final accounting —
     (verified fixed / discovered now / still open + why) + resources used
 [ ] ai_analysis/attempt-01/decision_log.md final entries written
STOP when the stop condition is met: no new lead deserves creation.
END OF WORKLOAD. Leave apktool/, surface/, trufflehog/, manifest/, prompts/,
handoff.json, hashes.sha256, and README.txt byte-identical to arrival (only
ai_analysis/attempt-01/ is created/modified, so bundle verification passes).
