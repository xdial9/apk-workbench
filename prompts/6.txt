6.txt — STAGE 6: CLOSURE — SET YOUR AGENTS FREE
================================================================================
GOAL
Assume you missed something. Prove that you did not. This is the final stage:
adversarial self-audit, free exploration, verification/fixing, and a clean
accountable finish.

PHASE A — ADVERSARIAL AUDIT (structured; must complete)
For each question, answer in ai_analysis/memory/closure_audit.md with
citations:
  - Traversal: did stage 1 truly cover 100% of the apktool/ tree? (spot
    check random directories not in your notes — do the deferred ones)
  - The ones that got away: any region you skipped as "boring"? Look again:
    assets, native .so strings, obscure res/ xml files, META-INF.
  - Cross-cutting misses: did any finding conclude WITHOUT its second
    half (e.g., endpoint found — was its source component ever traced?)
  - Stage 2 disputes: is every cross_check.md dispute resolved, or
    explicitly carried as an open question?
  - Lead ledger completeness: every entry has a status + reason?
  - Were there tool outputs you read only partially? (large facts.jsonl
    sweeps you skipped; do a targeted re-sweep by fact kind)
  - Anything in location_map.json you never opened?
Fix what the audit finds: open new mini-leads, settle them with stage-3
method, reflect in dossier/ai_analysis.json.

PHASE B — FREE EXPLORATION LICENSE (your agents, your rules)
If your environment permits spawning agents/workers, fan out across
unresolved threads now (one thread each; report back into memory/). If not,
iterate solo. Suggested free-hunting grounds (non-exclusive):
  - decode anything still undecoded (base64, protobuf, custom encodings)
  - enumerate version deltas if sibling artifact folders are accessible
    from the workspace you were handed (otherwise skip conditionally)
  - string-mine native libs for endpoints/keys (strings-equivalent scan)
  - call-graph hunting: pick 3 sensitive APIs and trace their call sites
    end-to-end; note where static mappings pay off.
Anything new -> new leads -> stage-3 method -> reflect in deliverables.

PHASE C — CONVERGENCE (required stop condition; linear/finishable doctrine)
The stage is DONE only when ALL of these are true:
 [ ] every lead in lead_ledger.json: pursued | dismissed (with reason)
 [ ] every coverage surface line: expanded by your work or marked unknown
     with an honest reason
 [ ] dossier.md + ai_analysis.json updated with everything found in Phase A/B
 [ ] closure_report.md written: final accounting — (verified fixed /
     discovered now / still open + why) + resources used
 [ ] decision_log.md final entries written
STOP when the stop condition is met: no new lead deserves creation.
END OF WORKLOAD. Leave the folder: apktool/, surface/, trufflehog/,
manifest/ byte-identical to arrival (proof of a clean run: you may keep
copies of anything inside ai_analysis/memory/).
