2.txt — STAGE 2: INTEGRATE THE PROGRAM'S OUTPUT (after your raw map is done)
================================================================================
GOAL
Read the deterministic pipeline's established facts and separate: (a) claims
you can confirm from your own raw read, (b) claims you DISAGREE with or find
incomplete, (c) things the tool marked as unknown. Group (b) and (c) are the
reasons you exist — record them prominently.

INPUTS
  surface/static-analysis.json   summary: facts, findings, coverage ledger,
                                 deductions, limitations
  surface/facts.jsonl            one line per extracted fact with full
                                 evidence (path/line/cited occurrences)
  surface/location_map.json      index of every entry (exact file:line)
  trufflehog/                    verified secret findings (evidence-grade)
  manifest/google-play-bundle.json (if present): delivery bundle components
  your stage-1 products: ai_analysis/memory/raw_map.md, my_leads.txt

METHOD
1. SKIM static-analysis.json section headers first (manifest_facts,
   inventory, findings kinds, deductions, coverage), then facts.jsonl only
   for kinds relevant to your leads. Do not dump the entire file into
   context: follow cited locations into apktool/ files when detail matters.
2. CONFIRMATION PASS: for each tool claim you sampled in stage 1 territory
   (permissions, components, endpoints, SDK inventory), verify against your
   raw map. Record agreement/in disagreement into
   ai_analysis/memory/cross_check.md:
     confirmed   — tool said X, my raw read agrees (cite both)
     disputed    — tool said X, raw read disagrees or contradicts (cite
                   both; this is a finding — create a lead
                   "reconcile: <topic>")
     missed      — tool has no record of something notable from stage 1
                   (also a finding/lead)
     expected_unknown — tool flagged unknown; judge if you can close it.
3. INVENTORY THE LEADS LEDGER: the tool's open leads live in
   surface/static-analysis.json under deductions/coverage (and location
   map entries). Merge them with your stage-1 my_leads.txt into
   ai_analysis/memory/all_leads.md (dedup by topic; keep citations).
   You will work through this merged list in stage 3.
4. TOOL-VERIFIED SECRETS: read trufflehog/ findings; cross reference each
   with your raw understanding (context of the file, what system uses it);
   note the usage context — verified secret + usage inference is one of
   the highest-value dossier items.
5. PROVENANCE + VERSION/DELIVERY NOTES: read google-play-bundle.json
   provenance (discovered_via, run_id, run manifest + verification report
   references) into NOTES.md — selection context the dossier identity
   section should carry. Note signer identity and
   whether this bundle had splits/asset packs not present in the artifact
   folder (coverage implication: some code/resources not analyzable here).

DEFINITION-OF-DONE
 [ ] cross_check.md written with confirmed + disputed + missed sections,
     every line cited
 [ ] all_leads.md merged and deduplicated (mine + tool's), each lead has a
     priority guess (high/med/low) and a one-line reason
 [ ] trufflehog findings read and usage-context annotated
 [ ] NOTES.md updated (stage 2 complete, notable agreements/disputes)

NOW READ 3.txt.
================================================================================
